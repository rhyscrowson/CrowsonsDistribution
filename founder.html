Meet the Founder

Rhys Crowson

I started Crowsons Global because I kept seeing the same problem from different angles: good stock passing through too many hands before it reached someone who could actually sell it properly, with cost and accountability getting diluted at every layer.

There's no call centre here and no automated quote system. When you get in touch, you're speaking to me directly, and I'm the one making the decision on pricing, terms and whether a partnership makes sense, from the first conversation through to your first order.

I'm building this deliberately, one proper relationship at a time, rather than chasing every opportunity that comes along. If that's the kind of trade partner you're looking for, I'd like to hear from you.
