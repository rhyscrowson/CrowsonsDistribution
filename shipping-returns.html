Shop policies

Delivery & Returns

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Delivery

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom. Standard delivery is £3.99, and free on orders of £30 or more.

Orders are packed and dispatched from the UK within 1 to 2 working days. Most parcels arrive 2 to 4 working days after dispatch. You'll get an email when your order ships.

Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're unused and in their original packaging. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you also have the right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it.

For hygiene reasons we can't accept returns of opened health and beauty products unless they're faulty.

To start a return, email trade@crowsonsglobal.com with your order email and the item you'd like to return. We'll reply with the return address.

Faulty or damaged items

If something arrives damaged or develops a fault, tell us within 30 days and we'll replace or refund it, including the cost of sending it back. Your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the return.
