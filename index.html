About Us

Who We Are

Crowsons Distribution started with a straightforward frustration: watching good stock move through too many hands before it ever reached someone who could actually sell it properly. Every extra layer added cost and pulled accountability further away from the people who needed it most, the buyers placing the order.

I built this business to close that gap. Every relationship starts with an actual conversation, not a portal or an automated quote, and when you call us, you're speaking to someone who can make a decision on the spot.

Our Vision

I see real scope for what this business can become across UK and European trade, not just here in Peterborough. Over the next few years, I want to keep building a network that brands trust with their name and buyers trust with their margin, growing deliberately rather than chasing every opportunity that comes along.

The goal isn't just to get bigger, it's to become the distribution partner other people in this industry measure themselves against.
