Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Our current position

This website, crowsonsglobal.com, operated by Crowsons Distribution Ltd, does not currently set any cookies: no analytics, no advertising or retargeting cookies, and no tracking of any kind. You can browse this site without a cookie consent banner appearing, because there is currently nothing on this site that requires your consent.

What we mean by "cookies"

A cookie is a small text file that a website can store in your browser to remember information between visits, such as your preferences or browsing behaviour. Websites also sometimes use similar technologies (like browser local storage or tracking pixels) for the same purpose. None of these are currently in use on this site.

The one external request this site makes

This site loads its fonts directly from Google Fonts' servers. Loading a font this way means your browser makes a request to Google, which may receive your IP address as a normal, technical part of serving that file, the same way any external resource works on the web. Google does not use this to set a cookie on this site, and we don't use it for tracking. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle personal data more broadly.

If this changes in future

If we introduce analytics, marketing, or any other cookie-based tool later on, we will update this policy to explain exactly what's used and why, and add a proper consent banner allowing you to accept or decline before anything is set. We won't add cookie-based tracking silently.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Even though this site doesn't currently use cookies, most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies from any site you visit, if you'd like to manage this generally:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
