Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Crowsons Global Ltd (company number 16709080) is the data controller for personal information collected through this shop.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Your email address if you sign up for our emails.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or by email.

How we use it

We use your details to process, deliver and support your order, to handle returns, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. If you sign up for emails, we'll send you offers and news; every email has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell your data.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes your payment, our delivery carriers receive your name and address to deliver your parcel, and Netlify hosts this website and our forms. Each handles your data under their own privacy policies and UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for six years for tax and accounting purposes, then delete them. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact us to do this. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your basket is saved in your own browser's local storage so it's still there when you come back; it never leaves your device until you check out. Stripe uses its own essential cookies on the payment page to prevent fraud.
