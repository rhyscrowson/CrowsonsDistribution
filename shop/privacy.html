Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Crowsons Global Ltd (company number 16709080) is the data controller for personal information collected through this shop.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Your basket contents, stored only in your own browser so it's there when you come back.

How we use it

We use your details to process, deliver and support your order, to handle returns, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We don't sell your data, and we won't send you marketing emails unless you've asked us to.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes your payment, our delivery carriers receive your name and address to deliver your parcel, and Netlify hosts this website and our contact form. Each handles your data under their own privacy policies and UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for six years for tax and accounting purposes, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact us to do this. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
