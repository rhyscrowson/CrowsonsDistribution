About us

Crowsons is an online shop for everyday home, beauty, toy and pet essentials, run from Peterborough by Crowsons Global Ltd.

We buy our stock from brand owners and authorised UK distributors, keep it in the UK, and pack every order ourselves. That means genuine products, sensible prices and a real person to talk to if anything goes wrong.

What we promise

  • Genuine stock from authorised suppliers
  • Orders dispatched within 1–2 working days
  • 30-day returns on most items
  • Replies to your messages within one working day
