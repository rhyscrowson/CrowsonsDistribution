Frequently asked questions

How much is delivery?

Standard UK delivery is £3.99, and free on orders over £30.

When will my order arrive?

We dispatch within 1–2 working days, and most parcels arrive 2–4 working days after that. We'll email you when your order ships.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We only deliver to UK addresses.

How do I track my order?

Your dispatch email includes tracking where the carrier provides it. If you can't find it, contact us with the email you ordered with.

How do I return something?

Most items can be returned within 30 days. See returns & refunds for how it works.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. We buy our stock from brand owners and authorised UK distributors.
