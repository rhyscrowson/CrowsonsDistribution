Delivery & returns

Delivery

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom. Standard delivery is £3.99, and free on orders of £30 or more.

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 working days. Most parcels arrive 2–4 working days after dispatch, and you'll get an email when your order ships.

Returns & refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're unused and in their original packaging. You also have the legal right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it.

For hygiene reasons we can't take back opened health and beauty products unless they're faulty.

To start a return, contact us with your order email and the item you'd like to return, and we'll send you the return address.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving your return.

Faulty or damaged items

If something arrives damaged or develops a fault, let us know within 30 days and we'll replace or refund it, including the cost of sending it back. This doesn't affect your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
